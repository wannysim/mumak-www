"It works" and "I know why it works" are not the same thing.
This is a collection of notes that digs into how JavaScript, as a language, actually runs on top of an engine.

The goal is to answer the "why?" questions: what happens on the call stack when you run a single line of code, how closures linger in memory,
and why `this` keeps changing from one moment to the next.
