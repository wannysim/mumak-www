I saw the PR porting React Compiler to Rust.
More than the 3-10x performance numbers, the author's one line -- "the majority was coded by AI" -- stayed with me longer.

It felt strange.
Right after governance was pried out of Meta's hands and made independent,
one of the biggest changes landed with almost no public discussion -- and written by AI, at that.

Both the objections and the support make sense, so I can't easily land on one side.
But one thing is clear: the real test isn't today, the day it merged, but six months from now, when a subtle bug shows up.
Whether a human can trace and fix that code directly will likely decide whether this experiment succeeds.
